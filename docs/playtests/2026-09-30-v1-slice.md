# Playtest note — V1 slice first deploy (2026-09-30)

Build: `2aa6ce8`, https://crew-neon-omega.vercel.app. Observations are from agents' headless
runs and Playwright sessions; the owner's first phone playtest is pending.

## Working end to end
- Freighter deck, 8 crew, needs, work, power/heat/faults/repair, travel between 3 ports, jobs and
  payment, decision cards, run-ahead playback, IndexedDB + Neon saves, narrator via OpenRouter.
- First live narrator call (approachingPort fixture, `anthropic/claude-sonnet-4.6`): 24.7 s,
  4.9k in / 0.8k out tokens, two `offerJob` commands within price bands, a named NPC
  ("Ysolde Maren, Meridian Growers Collective") and a bounded notes object. No dropped intents.

## Looked good
- Job card at 0:00; crew haul crates, eat, sleep in bunks; ticker has a steady flow of chat lines.
- Engineer hears the grinding pump, diagnoses (~20 s), repairs (~2 min).
- Two tired crew race for one bunk; the loser waits ~2 s then takes another.
- "Preparing to depart → Undocking → Under way" with the travel bar filling.

## Looked dumb (candidates for W4b / tuning)
- Per-actor selection: the captain abandons her meal to cross the ship for a repair while the
  engineer who diagnosed it wanders off to chat (scarce-skill cost, SD §15.3 step 4, is W4b).
- A job's cargo is one stack, so one person hauls everything while others idle.
- On-duty crew with nothing to do stand "idle" at their post for a minute or more.
- Captain shows "idle" beside the console in transit instead of "at the helm".
- Chat lines repeat and "complained about the docking fee" plays before any fee.
- Most hooks fire from quietTooLong (40 s); little narrative signal otherwise.
- Desktop ticker overlaps Bridge / Crew Quarters labels.
- Narrator latency ~25 s; no prompt-cache hits yet on the first call.
