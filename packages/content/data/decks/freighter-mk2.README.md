# Freighter Mk2 — deck layout

Single deck, 36 x 20 tiles, hull class `light_freighter`, size class M (D72, D73). East (high x)
is fore, west (low x) is aft; the quay is south. The layout is ship life §8
(`docs/design/ship-life.md`). The map below is generated by `crew content render-deck
deck.freighter_mk2`; regenerate it after any layout change.

The bridge, the officers' cabins and the airlock are at the bow; engineering is aft. The bridge
has exactly one door (`bridge_corridor`). Engineering is reachable two ways — via the corridor
(`engineering_corridor`) and via the cargo hold (`engineering_cargo`) — so a single jammed door
cannot isolate it. The corridor opens into the cross-passage at (30,8) and (31,8) with no door,
and the galley's serving hatch into the mess at (23,9) east is an opening, not a door.

Both ramps open in the south hull: `personnel_ramp` at (34,19) from the airlock (transfer tile
(34,18)) and `cargo_ramp` at (13,19) from the hold (transfer tile (13,18)).

Berths: the captain's and first officer's cabins hold one bunk each, the passenger cabin two, and
the bunkroom eight in two rows of four (columns 14, 15, 17 and 18, leaving column 16 clear from
the door). Every bunk has a one-tile `locker` zone whose `machineKey` names it. Nine crew spawn
points, none on a machine or an interaction tile; the relief pilot's is in the bunkroom.

Reserved: bridge tiles (29,1) and (29,4) are floor with nothing on them, held for the later
stations (ship life §6). The nav terminal, ops terminal, vid screen and engineering console draw
a little `domestic` power. The off-watch furniture placed in slice 1 is used from slice 4 (D84):
see "Off watch" below the map.

Intercoms (slice 5, D94): one `machine.intercom` per compartment, sixteen, each on a free tile
against a wall with its `panel` position on the room's floor, off every spawn point, zone and
interaction tile. A report at a panel reaches everyone aboard. The default scenario stows two
patch kits in each of the twelve bunk lockers (D93). The hull bar is 100 (`hull.max`).

## Off watch

Where each activity in `data/activities/` is held on this deck (D84): the venue alternatives the
deck has, in each activity's order (the sim takes the first present; ship life plan, slice 4 call
12). The validator (`validate-venues.ts`) checks that a scenario's deck hosts every activity and
that each venue seats the activity's `participants.max`; regenerate this list after a layout or
activity change.

- **Lounge**: cards (`games_table_1`, four seats; the lounge floor if the table is gone), board
  game (games table), vids and video game (`vid_screen_1`, four places), debate, music, reading
  (for anyone without a desk of their own), stargazing (`viewport`, two tiles; in transit only).
- **Mess**: debate and music, when the lounge is not on the deck.
- **Gym**: working out (`exercise_rig_1`), sparring (`sparring_mat`, four tiles).
- **Captain's Cabin**, **First Officer's Cabin**: reading at one's own desk.
- **Engineering**: tinkering (`workbench_1`, two places).
- **Galley**: baking (`galley_stove_1`); the treats go on the `serving` zone.

Books (`item.book` × 3) start on the `book_shelf` zone in the lounge and go back there after
reading; the harmonica lives in Bettina's locker beside her bunk.
